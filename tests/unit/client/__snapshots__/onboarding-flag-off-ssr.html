How Prediabetes Pal works

What to expect

  • When we're unsure, we say so — you'll see it in the result.
  • Guidance is qualitative — plain words, not glucose numbers or calorie math.
  • The labels describe general meal patterns. Your A1C range only makes the presentation more cautious; it does not predict your individual response.
  • It is information to decide with, not medical advice.

Prediabetes Pal is informational only and is not medical advice. Talk with a doctor or registered dietitian for guidance that is specific to you.
